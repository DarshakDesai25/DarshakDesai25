## 🔬 Research

**A Procedural Lens on Emergent Biases in Multimodal AI Systems**
Resmi Ramachandranpillai, **Darshak Kamlesh Desai**, Malihe Alikhani · *AAAI 2026, AI for Social Impact Track*

A framework that traces bias stage by stage through vision language pipelines like CLIP. It shows that debiasing image and text separately can work in some cases and fail in others, because new biases emerge when the two get combined.

**LLM Digital Twins Evaluation** · BIT Lab, Boston University

Evaluation pipelines and a 6 category error taxonomy for testing whether LLM based digital twins simulate individual human behavior. Models pull extreme human ratings toward the average in more than half of cases.
